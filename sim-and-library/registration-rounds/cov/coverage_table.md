# Empirical coverage of the deviation margin λ_l·σ̂_l (spec §4.102 item 4)

Generated by `experiment/qsim/cov/coverage.py` (read-only use of frozen `v8sim2.Prior`, `v8sim7.cfg_measured`); raw numbers in `coverage.json`.

**Definition (same as the planner's σ̂, `v8sim2.py:39-42`, `export_sigma.py:15-18` → daemon `prior_sigma.json`).** Rise R(t) = max(0, max_{τ∈[H_l−30 s, H_l]} D(t+τ) − D(t)): the peak over the *arrival window* (last 30-s tick) of the replenish horizon, not the max over (0, H_l] and not the endpoint. H_UP = 60 s (window [30,60] s), H_VY = 180 s ([150,180]), H_WK = 180 s ([150,180] = h_WK+h_UP+g). Margin M = λ·σ̂_win,l(D(t)), σ̂ = population std of R in 10-UE buckets of D(t) (≥30 samples, else global), linearly interpolated between bucket centres. Units UE (×250 kpps in the daemon; ratio-invariant). Exceed = R > M. In-sample (σ̂ fitted on the same 1615 traces). σ̂ is the std of a non-negative, mostly-zero variable taken about its mean, while the margin is measured from zero, so 1−Φ(λ) is only a nominal reference.

Rows: UP uses λ_UP; VY and WK use λ_VM (same λ, same H = 180 s but different σ̂ tables because WK's window starts at h_WK+h_UP=150 s and VY's at h_VY=150 s — identical windows here, so VY and WK coincide). `WK-slotpath` = the second term of the worker target, need_UP(D + λ_UP·σ̂_win,WK)/spw (`v8sim10.py:27`).

## Reading

- Empirical exceedance is roughly 10–37× the Gaussian nominal 1−Φ(λ) at S4a and 3.5–6× at S4b (per cell): the rise distribution is a point mass at 0 plus a heavy right tail, so λ·σ̂ is not a quantile. Hence the wording "deviation multiplier with empirically reported coverage".
- Margin exceedance is per decision point and is not the SLO risk the λ were calibrated to (0.5% / 2% of minutes in loss): need_l(·) rounds up to whole units, so a rise beyond the margin only costs SLO when it also crosses the next unit boundary before replenishment lands.
- 30–40 UE has the lowest exceedance because demand is capped at 40 UE (σ̂ of bucket 4 = 0 and interpolation shrinks σ̂ toward it above 35 UE, but rises there are also bounded). 20–29 UE is the worst bucket in every row.
- VY and WK rows coincide: both arrival windows are [150,180] s (h_VY = 150; h_WK + h_UP = 120 + 30), so their σ̂ tables are identical and they share λ_VM.
